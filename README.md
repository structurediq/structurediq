# StructuredIQ

**Healthcare Operations Intelligence · Pune, India**

We transform how your hospital operates. Not another HMIS.

We study how care actually moves through your hospital, redesign what slows it down, and make the improvement stick with technology — so your people spend less time on process and more time on patients.

StructuredIQ helps Indian hospitals run better: we redesign the workflows that slow care down and make the gains stick with HMIS, EMR, ABDM and FHIR.

**What we work on:** shorter waiting rooms · faster discharges · fewer claim rejections · bills that catch every service · less expired stock · doctors who actually use the EMR · paper-free wards · records that follow the patient.

**Our approach:** technology is the enabler, not the product. We observe how care really moves, identify the pain points, map existing workflows, redesign what's inefficient, configure the software only then, and measure and keep improving.
